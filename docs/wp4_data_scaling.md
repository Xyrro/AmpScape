# WP4 — data-scaling ablation at S (T1, seed 1)

Question (review addendum WP4): how much of the official baselines' error is a data limit, and how much a training
budget limit? U-Net and FNO on T1 at tier S were trained on n ∈ {1,000; 5,000; 20,000} landscapes in two regimes —
**fixed-epoch** (the official 30 epochs on the subset) and **fixed-step** (≈ the number of optimisation steps of the
full run, i.e. more epochs on the subset) — and compared with the official full run (61,577 QC-passing train
landscapes, 30 epochs). Rows: `docs/tables/baselines_full.md` (run names `*_n<k>_ep30` / `*_n<k>_steps`);
table and figure regenerated by `scripts/paper_figures.py` → `paper/tables/wp4_data_scaling.md`,
`paper/figures/wp4_data_scaling.png`. Test split: test_id at S; metric rel-L2 (lower is better).

| model | n train | fixed-epoch (30) | fixed-step |
|---|---|---|---|
| U-Net | 1,000 | 0.496 | 0.368 (88 epochs) |
| U-Net | 5,000 | 0.316 | 0.139 (143 epochs) |
| U-Net | 20,000 | 0.188 | 0.103 (94 epochs) |
| U-Net | 61,577 (official) | 0.111 | — |
| FNO | 1,000 | 0.575 | 0.438 (39 epochs) |
| FNO | 5,000 | 0.357 | 0.298 (54 epochs) |
| FNO | 20,000 | 0.243 | 0.246 (36 epochs) |
| FNO | 61,577 (official) | 0.198 | — |

Findings (seed 1 only; the fixed-step runs' epoch counts come from the time-budgeted legs, see the table):

1. **At a fixed step budget the U-Net saturates early in data:** 20,000 landscapes give rel-L2 0.103 and 5,000 give
   0.139, against 0.111 for the official run on 61,577 landscapes with the same step budget. The official 30-epoch
   protocol at S is therefore limited by optimisation budget more than by data for the U-Net; the extra 40,000
   landscapes buy nothing at that budget.
2. **The FNO keeps improving with data** in both regimes (0.438 → 0.298 → 0.246 → 0.198), with little difference
   between fixed-epoch and fixed-step above 5,000 landscapes: it is data-limited, not budget-limited, at S.
3. **Fixed-epoch training on small subsets understates both models** by 1.3–2.3× rel-L2 at 1,000–5,000 landscapes —
   the comparison regime matters when quoting data-efficiency.
4. Consequence for the paper: report the official rows as the protocol result and state that the S tier is not
   data-limited for convolutional models under this budget; the scale-dependence (S → M → L error growth, see
   `paper/tables/baselines_sml.md`) is therefore not explained by the smaller train sets at M/L alone — the
   fixed-step rows at M/L would be the direct test and are not part of this plan.

Cost: 12 runs, 5.1 GPU-h in total (`docs/tables/baselines_full.md`, train GPU-h column).
